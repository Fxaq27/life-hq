Privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026.

What Life HQ accesses

With the account owner's permission, Life HQ reads and writes data in the owner's own Google account: calendar events, a Google Sheet of tasks and shopping, order and invoice emails, and files in a Drive folder.

How the data is used

Only to organise the household: listing tasks, adding calendar reminders, summarising the day, and filing invoices. Nothing else.

Where the data goes

The data stays in the owner's Google account and on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI models. Text needed to answer a request may be processed by the AI model the owner has chosen for their assistant.

Google user data

Life HQ's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Life HQ's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored copies can be deleted from the owner's Google Drive and Sheet.

Contact

Use the developer contact email shown on the Google sign-in screen for this app.
