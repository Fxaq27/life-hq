Life HQ

A private household and small-business organiser used by one couple. It shows their shared tasks, shopping list, cake orders and calendar on a home-screen web app, and lets their personal assistant (running on their own computer) add reminders and calendar events for them.

Who can use it

Only the two owners of the household. It is not offered to the public and there is no sign-up.

What it connects to

The owners' own Google account: Calendar (to add reminders and events), Sheets (their shared task list), Gmail (to read order and invoice emails sent to the household) and Drive (to file invoices).
